PRACTICAL ADVICE

Simple Study Tips

Small changes to your study routine can make learning more organised and effective.

01

Set a clear goal

Before you start studying, decide exactly what you want to accomplish during the session.

02

Remove distractions

Put unnecessary notifications and distractions aside so you can give your attention to the material you are learning.

03

Take useful breaks

Short breaks can give you time to rest and return to your work with renewed attention.
